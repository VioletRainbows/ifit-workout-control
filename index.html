iFit Workout Control

How to Use

This app only works with older iFit treadmills that have an audio port.

  1. Plug your phone or device into the treadmill's iFit audio port.
  2. Make sure your phone or device sound is ON.
  3. Press the iFit button on the treadmill.
  4. Press Start below in this app — do not start the workout on the treadmill itself.
